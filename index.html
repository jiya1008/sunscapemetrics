ABOUT HEATGUARD

Turning climate data into
actionable intelligence.

HeatGuard is a web-based climate intelligence platform designed to help citizens and authorities understand heatwave conditions, identify high-risk areas and respond before extreme heat becomes dangerous.

🌡️

Monitor

Monitor temperature, humidity and other environmental conditions using continuously updated climate data.

🧠

Analyze

Analyze climate trends and classify heatwave conditions according to their potential severity.

⚠️

Warn

Deliver clear risk indicators and early warnings so communities can take timely action.
